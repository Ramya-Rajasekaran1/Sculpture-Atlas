Keep the gallery dark black and sculpture-focused, with a divine atmosphere and visible layered dust. Particles should move noticeably faster than the original slow-drift treatment. Sculpture 1 keeps gold dust; sculptures 2 and 3 use grey particles; sculpture 4 uses refined, muted colored particles. The main frame and each artwork thumbnail use matching artwork-specific border colors.

**Why:** The user requested a divine vibe, then explicitly asked for a dustier background, faster particles, a dark black background, and particle colors matching the numbered sculptures. Performance remains a priority.

**How to apply:** Favor background effects over foreground decoration. Make the particles visibly large, including their solid cores, not only their faint glow: the user repeated the request for bigger particles after the subtler enlargement. Keep the fourth artwork's colored dust muted and restrained. Match the main frame border to the selected work and each thumbnail border to its own work. Keep mobile effects lighter, honor reduced motion, and pause background motion when the page is hidden.

Use instrumental thillana-style background music, enabled by default, with an explicit on/off button.

**Why:** The user requested instrumental thillana audio, an on/off button, and then explicitly asked to keep music on by default.

**How to apply:** Keep music independent of sculpture selection and preserve the visitor's off choice. Where browsers block audible autoplay, start on a genuine interaction and show the blocked state honestly.

Skip the YouTube music link; keep the mellow instrumental thillana in the gallery instead.

**Why:** The user decided to skip the YouTube track and keep a mellow thillana.

**How to apply:** Keep the background-music toggle on the gallery’s instrumental track; do not add a YouTube player or external-link icon.

Give the third sculpture a black-steel material shade while preserving its original carved detail.

**Why:** The user requested a steel-like, black-colored finish for the third sculpture specifically.

**How to apply:** Keep that finish consistent across its thumbnail and all rotation views without changing the other sculptures' materials.
